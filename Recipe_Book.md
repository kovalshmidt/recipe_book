### Varenyky (Ukrainian Dumplings)
- **Ingredients**: Flour, eggs, water, salt, potatoes, onions, butter, sour cream (for filling and serving).
- **Instructions**: Make dough from flour, eggs, water, and salt. Let it rest. Prepare filling by mashing boiled potatoes with fried onions. Roll out dough, cut circles, add filling, and seal edges. Boil in salted water until they float. Serve with butter and sour cream.

### Holubtsi (Stuffed Cabbage Rolls)
- **Ingredients**: Cabbage leaves, ground meat (pork and beef mix), rice, onions, carrots, tomato sauce, salt, pepper, bay leaf.
- **Instructions**: Blanch cabbage leaves. Mix ground meat with cooked rice and fried onions. Wrap mixture in cabbage leaves. Place in a pot, cover with tomato sauce and water. Simmer for 1.5-2 hours until tender. Serve hot with sour cream.

### Borscht
- **Ingredients**: Beets, cabbage, potatoes, carrots, onions, tomato paste, garlic, beef or pork, bay leaf, salt, pepper, sour cream.
- **Instructions**: Boil the meat to make broth. Dice vegetables and sauté beets, carrots, and onions with tomato paste. Add cabbage and potatoes to the broth, then add sautéed vegetables. Simmer until tender. Serve hot with sour cream and garlic.

### Medivka
- **Ingredients**: Honey (200g), water (1 liter), hops or spices (optional: cinnamon, cloves, ginger), yeast (for fermented version).
- **Instructions**: Dissolve honey in warm water. For traditional fermented medivka, add yeast and let it ferment for several days. For simple version, mix honey with boiled water, add spices, and let cool. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
